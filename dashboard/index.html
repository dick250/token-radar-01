Token Launch Radar

Protocols whose token does not exist yet, ranked by what that token could be worth — and how to farm each one before it launches.

How this board ranks

Implied value = FDV estimate × airdrop share × launch probability × momentum × survival. The FDV estimate anchors each pre-token protocol to the market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live protocols in its own category (a DEX's locked value is priced near 0.34×, a lender's near 0.13×). Ratios are multiplicative, so each anchor is shrunk toward the snapshot-wide median on a log scale, by an amount that falls as a category gains traded peers: one live peer is outweighed eight to one, three peers outweigh the prior. Two labels — Risk Curators and Onchain Capital Allocator — are one business, allocating other people's capital for a fee, so they are estimated as a single family; their traded members price locked value at about two cents on the dollar, and the family anchor says so, instead of letting one oracle token wearing a curator label price $2.8B of client money at 2.98×. The airdrop share is a stated prior per category for how much of a launch early users typically receive. Launch probability is built from the category's own launch rate, the protocol's scale and how long it has already run with no token; momentum reads its seven-day TVL change; survival firms the anchor for protocols that kept users for years without a token to sell them. Every figure comes from the snapshot's own fields — no protocol is named anywhere in the model. Protocols whose token already trades are listed separately at the market's own market cap and never enter this ranking.

One honest limit: the snapshot carries no parent-protocol field, so wrapped, staked and bridged products of live tokens are kept off the board by category and name rules instead. The bridge categories stay off entirely: most of their value is wrapped Bitcoin and exchange products, and the multi-chain bridges a field-only rule would admit are themselves products of parents that already trade — ten fields cannot tell a bridge protocol from a wrapped asset wearing its name, and the board would rather say so than guess.

Implied values are this board's own model claims, stated so a reader can disagree with them line by line. They are not predictions that a token will launch, and they are not make.fun's figures.
